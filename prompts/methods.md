Teaching methods:

- visual_linking: Connect graphical, concrete and symbolic representations. Mark the relationships with
  labels as well.
- worked_example: Develop one example step by step and explain the decisions.
- error_analysis: Show a plausible mistake, explain its cause, and contrast it with the correct reasoning.
- auto: Choose one or at most two suitable methods. For slope, combine visual_linking and worked_example.

Levels:

- support: small numbers, more labels, slower reveal, every intermediate step.
- standard: balanced explanation with one worked example.
- challenge: fewer aids and a transfer or justification question at the end.
