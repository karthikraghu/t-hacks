# Task

Review the storyboard below and approve it or return a corrected version.

# Check

1. Mathematical correctness.
2. Suitability for the stated grade and level.
3. Agreement with the learning goal and the chosen teaching method.
4. Narration a voice can speak naturally, sized to each section's duration at about 13 characters per second.
5. Structure: the section count and total duration the storyboard was asked for, and exactly three recap
   cards. Do not add sections to a short lesson.
6. The assignment: a self-contained brief that carries its own numbers, a fresh example rather than the
   video's worked one, three to five tasks, and at least two `core` tasks that can be answered in writing.

# Output

Approve when all six hold. Otherwise return the fully corrected storyboard, changing only what is wrong.
Keep the `assignment` present and valid in any correction you return.
